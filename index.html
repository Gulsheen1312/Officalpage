About us

Learning French, made simple

At GFC – Gulsheen French Classes, we believe a language is learned by using it. Every lesson combines clear grammar, real vocabulary and plenty of speaking, so you actually remember and use what you learn.

Why students choose us

  • Small batches with individual attention
  • Clear, step-by-step teaching
  • Regular practice, feedback and revision
  • Flexible timings, online and in person
  • Support through every level, up to exams
